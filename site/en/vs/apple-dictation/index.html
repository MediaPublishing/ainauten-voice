Features in your workflow

Enable Dictation in Keyboard settings and pick a shortcut. This gives you a real baseline without another installer. Only add an app trial when vocabulary, text modes or history solve a concrete missing step.

Local text modes, vocabulary, optional history and statistics support drafting. Automatic insertion does not use the general clipboard by default; compatible insertion is an explicit option. A free download does not replace a compatibility test.

Compare costs fairly

The additional app price is zero for one person or ten existing Mac workstations. This is not a total-cost calculation for hardware, IT or support.

The app price remains zero for ten downloads. IT effort, devices, storage and support are not included; this implies no enterprise service commitment.

When Apple Dictation is a better fit

For occasional dictation without installing another app, try Apple Dictation first. Apple points to separate Voice Control for broader hands-free control.

Not all languages offer the same features. Dictation and Voice Control are different workflows; choose by task and check the privacy notice for your configuration.

When AInauten Voice fits

AInauten Voice fits local dictation on an Apple Silicon Mac if you accept the limits of a free beta.

The beta is locally signed, not Apple-notarized, and uses a library-validation exception. You must approve the first launch in macOS yourself. This is not a blanket security approval. Automatic insertion without the clipboard does not work in every field.

Switching and setup

There is no Apple Dictation import. Configure a different shortcut so both features do not start together. Test a non-sensitive text field first and keep macOS dictation as your baseline.

Keep your existing workflow during the trial. Install the beta using the guide, approve microphone and accessibility permissions yourself and configure a separate shortcut. Test one sentence, specialist vocabulary and then a longer paragraph. Rely on insertion in your destination app only after that check.

Support and evidence

Our beta has help and a reporting option. It does not promise guaranteed enterprise support or full validation of every destination app. Check the other vendor documentation for contractual support terms.

A common question

Is AInauten Voice universally better than Apple Dictation?

No. Decide by devices, data routes, required features and correction effort. We have measured no general accuracy advantage here.
