When VoiceInk is a better fit

VoiceInk is the stronger shortlist choice if you want local dictation and an explicitly licensed open-source codebase to inspect or build.

The paid Mac download has device limits. Cloud enhancement is a separate data route; locally recognized speech does not make subsequent cloud processing local.

When AInauten Voice fits

AInauten Voice fits local dictation on an Apple Silicon Mac if you accept the limits of a free beta.

The beta is locally signed, not Apple-notarized, and uses a library-validation exception. This is not a blanket security approval. Automatic insertion without the clipboard does not work in every field.

Our product evidence: README · User verification status.

Switching and setup

We do not offer a direct VoiceInk migration. Move specialist vocabulary and shortcuts individually, retest text modes and check the target app. An existing VoiceInk license does not transfer.

Keep your existing workflow during the trial. Install the beta using the guide, approve microphone and accessibility permissions yourself and configure a separate shortcut. Test one sentence, specialist vocabulary and then a longer paragraph. Rely on insertion in your destination app only after that check.

Support and evidence

Our beta has help and a reporting option. It does not promise guaranteed enterprise support or full validation of every destination app. We publish no invented switching stories or attributed customer quotes in this comparison. Check the other vendor documentation for contractual support terms.

A common question

Is AInauten Voice universally better than VoiceInk?

No. Decide by devices, data routes, required features and correction effort. We have measured no general accuracy advantage here.
