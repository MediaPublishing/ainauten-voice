What makes switching worthwhile

A Superwhisper alternative should offer more than a lower price. If prompts and model combinations drive your workflow, first test whether a simpler local workflow actually covers it.

Your workflow is the relevant difference. A free download is useful when it covers your task. Price cannot compensate for unsupported devices or missing capabilities you require.

What you can move

We do not offer a direct Superwhisper migration. Add important vocabulary deliberately and configure shortcuts and text modes again. Back up custom prompts separately and compare their effects on examples.

Our product evidence: README · User verification status.

Trial first, switch later

Record three things your current tool handles well: a typical text field, a recurring specialist term and a longer text. Repeat exactly those tasks with the beta. Check meaning, paragraphs and insertion. If any task works worse, keep your existing workflow for that task.

The beta is locally signed, not Apple-notarized, and uses a library-validation exception. This is not a blanket security approval. Automatic insertion without the clipboard does not work in every field.

Cancel a subscription and remove the old app only after comparing. Back up vocabulary and drafts using existing exports. A trial import does not prove that every field or setting transferred.

When to keep your existing tool

Superwhisper is the stronger shortlist choice for extensive model selection and custom prompts. Verify local mode on your actual device.

Different tasks can use different instructions and models. This is useful when you deliberately separate raw transcription, translation and rewriting. Our free starting point is aimed more at everyday local dictation.

What switching costs

Annual Pro costs USD 84.99 per person per year; ten separate annual licenses total USD 849.90. Twelve monthly payments cost USD 101.88 per person. This is not an enterprise quote; taxes and your own API usage are excluded.

The app price remains zero for ten downloads. IT effort, devices, storage and support are not included; this implies no enterprise service commitment.
