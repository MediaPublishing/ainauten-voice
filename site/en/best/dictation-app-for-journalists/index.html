The actual job

After an interview you need searchable text; while writing you need a quick rough draft. These are different product capabilities. Source work also requires tracing a statement back to the original; a clean sentence is not a verified quotation.

What matters

  • Evaluate file transcription and your own dictation separately.
  • Check quotations against the original recording.
  • Consider source protection across the entire data route.
  • Test names, places and numbers deliberately.

A suitable workflow

Keep the original recording in your approved archive. Use a transcript for searching but verify published quotations against the original. Dictate your own connecting prose separately so raw sources and editorial wording remain distinct.

Your practical comparison

Use an authorized sample recording with two speakers and a paragraph you dictate yourself. Check exports, retrieval and proper names. Compare correction effort for each workflow. A dictation shortcut is not an archival process.

When to choose differently

For vulnerable sources, “local” alone is insufficient. Check optional cloud steps, retention and device security. Our beta is not Apple-notarized; if newsroom policy disallows that, exclude it.

A common question

Can I publish optimized text as a quotation?

Not unchecked. Polishing may change wording. Direct quotations need verification against the original and your editorial standards.
