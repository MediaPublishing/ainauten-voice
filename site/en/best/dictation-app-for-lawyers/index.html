Dictation apps for lawyers: assess Mac use

Locally configured VoiceInk or Superwhisper workflows can be evaluated for non-confidential drafts. AInauten Voice is only a beta test candidate with known security limits. Firm approval, not our ordering, determines suitability for real client data.

The actual job

You dictate a draft with names, deadlines and an explicit negation. One wrong date or missing “not” may matter more than a hundred correctly recognized words. The data route also extends beyond the microphone: history, clipboard, destination software and cloud rewriting all matter.

What matters

  • Establish data routes and retention before use.
  • Check names, deadlines and negations individually.
  • Configure cloud enhancement and clipboard access deliberately.
  • Meet the firm's approval and security requirements.

A suitable workflow

Test only fictional matters first. Dictate into an appropriate draft area and verify every legally relevant value before using it. A checked template is safer than freely rewritten language for recurring wording. Local processing alone is not a compliance assessment.

Your practical comparison

Use a fictional name, date, amount and negation. Check where text is retained and whether the app works without a cloud connection. Have IT assess the specific setup afterwards. Vendor statements do not replace that review.

When to choose differently

Our beta is not Apple-notarized and uses a library-validation exception. If your firm disallows that configuration, AInauten Voice is unsuitable. We also make no blanket data-protection or professional-compliance approval claim for competitors.

A common question

Is local dictation software automatically suitable for a law firm?

No. Local processing is one part of the data and security design. Review the actual software, retention, destination application and firm requirements.
