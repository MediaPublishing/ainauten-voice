The actual job

You switch between proposals, client replies and project briefs. The tone may change, but prices, dates and promised deliverables must not. The cheapest app can cost more if every result takes substantial editing.

What matters

  • Check prices, dates and scope before sending.
  • Test email and informal chat as different writing tasks.
  • Calculate subscriptions, one-time purchases and possible API fees separately.
  • Assess confidentiality against the actual client engagement.

Options to evaluate first

AInauten Voice

On one suitable Mac, first test whether the free workflow covers mail drafting.

Free local dictation beta for Apple Silicon Macs.

Free, no subscription. Model downloads and device storage are part of setup; donations are optional.

A one-time purchase is worth considering if you want to avoid recurring app fees.

Local Mac dictation with a one-time purchase and openly licensed source code.

Solo: USD 25 for one Mac. Personal: USD 39 for two Macs. Extended: USD 49 for three Macs. One-time purchase; check team offers separately. Cloud text enhancement may involve provider fees.

Wispr Flow

For client work across phones and operating systems, device coverage matters more than a zero price.

Cross-device dictation with team features.

Free: USD 0, 2,000 words/week on desktop and 1,000 on mobile. Pro: USD 15/person/month or USD 12/month billed annually. Growth and Enterprise add packages; check prices and bundles at checkout.

A suitable workflow

Dictate the content of a proposal before adding binding figures. Enter amounts and dates carefully. Test email and chat styles using the same information and choose the workflow that preserves content. Sending remains your decision.

Your practical comparison

Create three fictional messages: a proposal, a rescheduled appointment and a project update. Check tone, amount, date and insertion in your mail app. Record correction time. Then compare cost with actual benefit instead of assuming a fixed time saving.

When to choose differently

If you work extensively on a phone or Windows, a Mac beta will not replace the whole workflow. Client requirements for approved software take priority. We promise neither fixed time savings nor automatic approval for client data.

A common question

Can a paid tool be worth more than the free beta?

Yes, if it reduces correction effort on your devices and destination apps or supplies features you need. Compare workflows, not just list prices.
