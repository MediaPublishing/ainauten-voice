# Competitive content: AInauten Voice

Brief: five relevant alternatives, five vs pages and five singular alternative pages, ten audience guides. Each exists in German and English: 40 articles plus two language hubs. Research date: 2026-10-09.

Selection: Wispr Flow, Superwhisper, VoiceInk (tryvoiceink.com / Beingpax, not the similarly named voice-ink.com), MacWhisper, Apple Dictation (built-in substitute, not a standalone vendor). Selected for task overlap, not market-share or keyword-volume ranking. No measured search volume available. Publish direct Wispr comparisons first in navigation, then offline options, built-in substitute and task-specific guides.

AC-1: 40 complete, non-placeholder articles; language-equivalent content, reciprocal hreflang and canonical URLs.
AC-2: dated centralized competitor profiles, direct primary-source links, no invented experience, testimonials, accuracy benchmarks or unsupported negative feature claims.
AC-3: each comparison recommends the competitor in a concrete situation; each guide has distinct audience criteria, practical test and disqualifiers. AInauten beta and security limitations remain visible.
AC-4: reading-first layout within existing AInauten colors, Inter, local assets and shared shell. Compact decision summary, table, detailed reasoning, switching and relevant links; no imitation of competitor branding. Functional without JavaScript. Desktop and mobile screenshots checked.
AC-5: build integration, sitemap, homepage discovery, link/metadata checks, source commit and live readback. Existing installer, update feed and reporting routes preserved byte-for-byte.

Non-goals: no app release, app settings changes, new accounts/trials/payments, medical or legal compliance certification, customer emails, analytics or automation. No fabricated review mining or buyer interviews. Methodology explicitly distinguishes vendor facts from editorial recommendations.

Budget: static files under 5 MB; no native build, model downloads or full artifact copies. At most three QA repair cycles. Existing 10 GiB disk reserve precludes native rebuilds. Deployment authorized under existing website standing approval after QA.

Design: a reader compares tools during the workday. Existing light/dark tokens, asymmetric reading column plus compact contents navigation; own product image only where useful. Vs pages start with tradeoffs and a table; alternatives start with switching constraints; guides start with a task-specific recommendation and practical workflow. No decorative animations or generic equal-card feature grid.

Layout owner: marketing-skills:competitors with existing PRODUCT.md / DESIGN.md and Impeccable + mp-design. External X post is task inspiration, not proof of revenue or a ranking. Referenced Rhino page could not be retrieved; no pixel copy.
